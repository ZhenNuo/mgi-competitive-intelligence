## 📐 评分标准

### 覆盖率（25%）

| 分数 | 核心竞品覆盖率 | 渠道覆盖率 | 细分领域覆盖 |
|------|---------------|-----------|-------------|
| 5 | ≥95% | ≥90% | ≥80% |
| 4 | ≥80% | ≥75% | ≥60% |
| 3 | ≥60% | ≥50% | ≥40% |
| 2 | ≥40% | ≥30% | ≥20% |
| 1 | <40% | <30% | <20% |

**计算公式**: `覆盖率得分 = (竞品覆盖×0.4 + 渠道覆盖×0.3 + 细分覆盖×0.3) × 5`

---

### 时效性（25%）

| 分数 | T+1 更新率 | 平均延迟 | 关键动态延迟 |
|------|-----------|---------|-------------|
| 5 | 100% | <12 小时 | <6 小时 |
| 4 | ≥80% | <24 小时 | <12 小时 |
| 3 | ≥60% | <48 小时 | <24 小时 |
| 2 | ≥40% | <72 小时 | <48 小时 |
| 1 | <40% | ≥72 小时 | ≥48 小时 |

**计算公式**: `时效性得分 = (T+1 率×0.5 + (72-平均延迟)/72×0.3 + (72-关键延迟)/72×0.2) × 5`

---

### 可信度（30%）

| 分数 | A 级数据占比 | A+B 级占比 | 交叉验证率 |
|------|-------------|-----------|-----------|
| 5 | ≥50% | ≥90% | ≥80% |
| 4 | ≥35% | ≥75% | ≥60% |
| 3 | ≥20% | ≥60% | ≥40% |
| 2 | ≥10% | ≥40% | ≥20% |
| 1 | <10% | <40% | <20% |

**计算公式**: `可信度得分 = (A 级占比×0.4 + (A+B) 级占比×0.4 + 交叉验证率×0.2) × 5`

---

### 可操作性（20%）

| 分数 | 行动建议数量 | RACI 明确率 | 截止时间明确率 | 资源需求明确率 |
|------|-------------|-----------|---------------|---------------|
| 5 | ≥5 条 | 100% | 100% | ≥80% |
| 4 | ≥4 条 | ≥80% | ≥80% | ≥60% |
| 3 | ≥3 条 | ≥60% | ≥60% | ≥40% |
| 2 | ≥2 条 | ≥40% | ≥40% | ≥20% |
| 1 | <2 条 | <40% | <40% | <20% |

**计算公式**: `可操作性得分 = (建议数/5×0.25 + RACI 率×0.3 + 截止率×0.3 + 资源率×0.2) × 5`

## 🔧 自动化评估脚本

```python
# 情报质量自动评估（伪代码）
def calculate_quality_score(report_data):
    # 覆盖率
    competitor_coverage = count_tracked_competitors() / TOTAL_COMPETITORS
    channel_coverage = count_active_channels() / TOTAL_CHANNELS
    
    # 时效性
    t1_rate = count_t1_updates() / total_updates
    avg_delay = average_delay_hours()
    
    # 可信度
    a_rate = count_a_grade_sources() / total_sources
    ab_rate = count_ab_grade_sources() / total_sources
    cross_verify_rate = count_cross_verified() / total_claims
    
    # 可操作性
    action_count = count_action_items()
    raci_rate = count_raci_assigned() / action_count
    
    # 计算各维度得分
    coverage_score = (competitor_coverage * 0.4 + channel_coverage * 0.3) * 5
    timeliness_score = (t1_rate * 0.5 + (72-avg_delay)/72 * 0.5) * 5
    credibility_score = (a_rate * 0.4 + ab_rate * 0.4 + cross_verify_rate * 0.2) * 5
    actionability_score = (min(action_count/5, 1) * 0.25 + raci_rate * 0.75) * 5
    
    # 综合得分
    total_score = (coverage_score * 0.25 + timeliness_score * 0.25 + 
                   credibility_score * 0.30 + actionability_score * 0.20)
    
    return {
        'coverage': coverage_score,
        'timeliness': timeliness_score,
        'credibility': credibility_score,
        'actionability': actionability_score,
        'total': total_score,
        'stars': score_to_stars(total_score)
    }
```
